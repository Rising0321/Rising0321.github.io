EN

My research interests lie in world model, generative model, and large Spatiotemporal models. Since I was a child, I have enjoyed exploring and wandering endlessly in both games and the real world. Even when playing shooting games, I just like to find a car to drive around the map. I hope that through the efforts of academia and myself, more people can wander freely in infinitely generated worlds.

中文

我的研究兴趣点在于 世界模型, 生成式模型 以及时空大模型. 我从小就喜欢在游戏和现实世界里无限探索和徜徉，就算玩枪战类游戏，我也只是喜欢找个车在地图里逛逛。我希望通过学术界和我的努力，能让更多的人在无限生成的世界里徜徉。

EN

Unfortunately, my research career is not peace. I, as the first author, after one paper being luckily accepted(IJCAI2022), have been rejected six times in a row (KDD2023, AAAI2024, KDD2024, IJCAI204, NeurIPS2024*2). After another one paper being accepted(KDD2025 Aug), another three were rejected(AAAI2025, ICML2025, KDD2025 Feb, AAAI2026, KDD2026 Aug). Then I was lucky to be accepted(ACL 2026 Findings) again. After that, another one paper was rejected(KDD2026 Feb). This may be related to my previous lack of focus on a research direction. I hope that both myself and you who see this homepage will be more determined and lucky.

Marathons（参加过的马拉松）

中文

我很喜欢跑步，因为跑步会分泌多巴胺，会让我感觉到快乐。同时，我也参与了西安怪兽跑团，跑团教会我一个人跑步心情是一种颜色，一群人跑步心情汇聚成一道彩虹。在跑团中，不断的认识新的朋友，不断的帮助新人开始他们的第一次跑步，也是一件很快乐的事情。幸运的是，虽然我的学术生涯很不幸，而且我对跑步也懈怠了很久，但是从2025年的夏天开始，我重新开始坚持锻炼，我终于在2025年的西安重新开始跑马拉松并获得了PB，接着我跑了2026年的无锡马拉松，希望我们能常在跑道上相见。

EN

I really enjoy running because it releases dopamine, which makes me feel happy. At the same time, I'm also part of the Xi'an Monster Crew Running, where I learned that running alone gives you one color of emotion, while running with a group brings together a whole rainbow of feelings. In the crew, I continuously meet new friends and help newcomers start their first run, which is also something that brings me great joy. Fortunately, although my academic journey has been full of setbacks and I neglected running for a long time, I started training again in the Summer of 2025. I finally returned to marathon running in Xi'an in 2025 and achieved a new personal best. Then I ran the Wuxi Marathon in 2026, and I hope we'll often meet each other on the track.
