Selected Talks（精选报告）

中文

作为一个博士生，常常需要汲取最前沿的知识，保持知识的不断更新。而遗憾的是，我并没有系统的获取前沿知识的渠道。所以我会自己逼着自己做一些前沿知识的讲解，放在B站上。因为学习并给人们讲清楚的过程，能够反哺自己学习。如果有明显的错误的话，网友们还可以给我指正。是一个很好的学习方法。这里分了两部分，一部分是我做得前沿知识的分享，一部分是我自学论文写作和因果学习（没学明白，已经放弃了）的视频，希望这些视频能够帮助到你，也欢迎你来批评指正。

EN

As a Ph.D. student, I often need to learn about the latest research and keep my knowledge up to date. Unfortunately, I don't have a systematic way of keeping up with new developments. So I push myself to explain what I've been learning in videos on Bilibili. Learning something and explaining it clearly to others helps deepen my own understanding, and viewers can point out any obvious mistakes. I've found this a great way to learn. This section has two parts: my talks on research frontiers, and videos from my self-study of scientific paper writing and causal inference (I never quite got my head around the latter and have since given up). I hope you find these videos helpful, and I welcome your feedback and corrections.

Marathons（参加过的马拉松）

中文

我很喜欢跑步，因为跑步会分泌多巴胺，会让我感觉到快乐。同时，我也参与了西安怪兽跑团，跑团教会我一个人跑步心情是一种颜色，一群人跑步心情汇聚成一道彩虹。在跑团中，不断的认识新的朋友，不断的帮助新人开始他们的第一次跑步，也是一件很快乐的事情。幸运的是，虽然我的学术生涯很不幸，而且我对跑步也懈怠了很久，但是从2025年的夏天开始，我重新开始坚持锻炼，我终于在2025年的西安重新开始跑全程马拉松，接着我跑了2026年的无锡马拉松和西安马拉松，希望我们能常在跑道上相见。

EN

I really enjoy running because it releases dopamine, which makes me feel happy. At the same time, I'm also part of the Xi'an Monster Crew Running, where I learned that running alone gives you one color of emotion, while running with a group brings together a whole rainbow of feelings. In the crew, I continuously meet new friends and help newcomers start their first run, which is also something that brings me great joy. Fortunately, although my academic journey has been full of setbacks and I neglected running for a long time, I started training again in the Summer of 2025. I finally returned to running full marathons in Xi'an in 2025, followed by the Wuxi and Xi'an Marathons in 2026. I hope we'll often see each other out on the course.
